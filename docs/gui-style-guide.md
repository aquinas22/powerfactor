# PowerFactor GUI style guide

"Bright high-tech": a pearl-white chassis with dark-glass displays set into it. These rules exist so every
screen feels made by one hand: aligned to one grid, balanced, and readable at a glance. The drawing kit is
`client/Skin.java`; the layout constants live in `Skin.Layout`. If a rule here and the kit disagree, fix the kit.

## 1. The grid

All measurements are GUI pixels relative to the screen's top-left corner.

```
 0 ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │▌ Title                            ● lamp │   title band: text y=6, divider y=16
16 ├──────────────────────────────────────────┤
20 │  content zone                            │   CONTENT_TOP = 20
   │  x 7 … w-7, aligned to 18-px columns     │
   │                                          │
   │                                          │   content ends at INV_Y - 16 (inclusive gap of 3 px
   ├──────────────────────────────────────────┤   above the divider)
   │ Inventory                                │   divider INV_Y - 13, label INV_Y - 11
INV_Y  ▢▢▢▢▢▢▢▢▢  (3 rows)                    │   INV_Y = h - 82 on every container screen
   │ ▢▢▢▢▢▢▢▢▢  hotbar at INV_Y + 58           │
 h └──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

* **Widths.** 176 (standard) or 248 (wide, for benches and terminals). Nothing else.
* **Heights.** 166 is standard (content zone y 20–68: 48 px). Taller screens add room in whole slot rows where
  they can (184, 202…); the console family is 190 (zone 20–92: 72 px). Whatever the height, the inventory sits at
  h − 82 and `Skin.Layout.contentBottom(h)` is the floor.
* **Horizontal margins.** Content lives between x=7 and x=w−7, the outer edges of the inventory grid. The
  162 px between is divided into 18 px columns (9 columns on a standard screen). Panels, slots and buttons
  start and end on column edges or column centres.
* **The energy gauge.** When a machine stores FE, its gauge is the rightmost element: x = w−20, 12 wide (ending
  8 px from the edge), from `CONTENT_TOP` to the bottom of the content zone. Never shorter, never floating.
* **Spacing unit.** 2 px. Gaps inside a group are 2 or 4; gaps between groups are 6 or 8. Never 1, 3, 5 or 7
  except for the 1-px lips that the kit draws itself.

## 2. Composition

* **Symmetry first.** A screen with one focal element (a ring, a bay, a radar) centres it on the panel's
  vertical axis (x = w/2) or on the content zone's centre line. Flows run left to right: inputs left, the
  process (chevrons, ring, beam) in the middle, outputs right, mirrored about the process.
* **Pairs balance.** Two side-by-side panels share a top edge and a bottom edge, even if one has less to say.
* **Everything sits in something.** Readouts live on dark glass (`Skin.lcd`); controls are dark chips
  (`Skin.control`); slots are wells. No loose light-on-light text in the content zone, except section
  labels.
* **One focal point.** Each screen has one hero element (the graphic that shows what the machine is
  doing), drawn largest, near the centre and animated. Everything else supports it.

## 3. Text

* **Show, don't tell.** If an icon can say it, use the icon: a lightning bolt beside a number instead of "FE",
  a droplet instead of "Fluid", a feather for silk touch, a bin for void. Every icon has a tooltip that names
  it, so nothing is lost; text is for numbers, names and the one-line status. Icons come from one hand-drawn
  set (`Skin.Icon`, generated by `tools/gui_icons.py`) so they share a palette, weight and 10 px grid.
* **Case.** Interface text is Title Case: every word starts with a capital except the articles a, an and
  the ("Mod Bench", "Waiting For Night", "Not On a Grid"). Explanatory sentences (tooltip help, the handbook)
  stay in sentence case. Capitals only for real acronyms (FE, EE, PF, SCADA, RPM, NAND); units keep their own
  case (mB/t, kV, m/s). `tools/text_style.py` applies this to the language file and `check_resources.py`
  rejects text that breaks it.
* **Fit by writing, not by cutting.** Wording is written to fit its space. `Skin.fit` (ellipsis) is the
  last resort and anything it cuts must be readable in full in a tooltip. Titles shrink up to 15% before
  they are ever cut.
* **On dark glass.** Text insets 4 px from the left and 3 px from the top of an LCD; lines are 10 px apart.
  An LCD of height h holds (h − 4) / 10 lines, and `Skin.lcdLines` draws only what fits, so a line is never
  half-clipped by the glass edge.
* **Colour.** On the chassis: `TEXT` for content, `DIM` for labels. On glass: `LCD_TEXT` / `LCD_DIM`, with
  the unit colours (FE coral, EE teal) and status colours (green running, amber waiting, red fault). Status
  text is a single short phrase; detail goes in the tooltip.

## 4. Controls

* **Chips.** `Skin.control`: 14 px tall (small) or 18 px (large, toggles with a lamp and two lines). Labels
  centred, with no text touching the rim. Rows of chips share widths and 2-px gutters.
* **Steppers.** −10 −1 +1 +10 is one group of four 20-px chips with 2-px gutters (86 px), right-aligned in
  its row with its value label on the left. A single value stepper is [−] value-glass [+] across the full column.
* **Chip labels** condense to fit (`Skin.condensed`) rather than being cut; if a label still doesn't fit, the
  wording changes.
* **Hover.** Every control and every gauge has a tooltip that says what it does and what it shows now.

## 5. Motion

* Animation means something: progress, flow, charge, activity. Idle screens are calm; working screens move.
* Animation speed follows the real rate where there is one (flow arrows speed up with throughput).
* Nothing blinks faster than 2 Hz. Alarms pulse; they don't strobe.

## 6. Checklist for a new or changed screen

1. Width 176 or 248, height 166 + 18n; inventory at h − 82.
2. Nothing in the content zone below `Skin.Layout.contentBottom(h)`.
3. Every element's edges land on the 2-px grid and on the 18-px column rhythm where they can.
4. Hero element centred; inputs left, outputs right; paired panels share edges.
5. Icons before words; Title Case labels; no all-caps words, no half-clipped lines, no ellipsis without a tooltip.
6. Capture it in the test client (`audit.py` in the agent scratchpad or `open_machine` + `capture_screen`)
   and look at it at GUI scale 2 before calling it done.
